# SE3002 Quality Evaluation - Part 1: Requirement Scope and AI Assumptions

**Course:** SE3002 Software Quality Engineering  
**Project:** VIPER Supply Chain Management (SCM) System (Ejada Case Study 2008)  
**Evaluation Standard:** SE3002 Assignment #01 Rubric (CLO1 - K2 Understand / K4 Analyze)  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Selection Strategy
Per the SE3002 Assignment #01 specifications, exactly 10 requirements were selected from the VIPER 2008 SRS document (`viper.doc`). The selection strictly satisfies the mandatory distribution:
- **7 Functional Requirements (70%)**: Comprising exactly **3 pure CRUD entities** (Requests, Customers, Items) and **4 high-value behavioral/workflow requirements** (Domain Authentication & Routing, Supplier Feedback Workflow, Request Modification Alerts, and Invalid Login Redirection).
- **3 Non-Functional Requirements (30%)**: Comprising **Performance (SRS 3.3)**, **Security RBAC (SRS 3.6.3)**, and **Availability / Fault Tolerance (SRS 3.6.2)**.

All AI-assisted implementation assumptions introduced during development have been explicitly documented, defended, and classified according to their defensible basis:
1. **Supported by SRS**: Directly anchored in explicit SRS text, use cases, or screen layouts.
2. **Justified by Design Decision**: An intentional architectural/engineering decision required to bridge underspecified 2008 legacy assumptions into a modern web baseline.
3. **Unsupported**: Documented gaps where AI generated behavior lacks explicit SRS ground truth.

## 3. Defense & Compliance Analysis

### 3.1 Compliance with CRUD Quota
The assignment instructions mandate that **no more than 3 of the 7 FRs may be pure CRUD requirements**.
- **CRUD Entity 1:** `FR-01` (Manage Requests - Add, View, Edit, Delete).
- **CRUD Entity 2:** `FR-02` (Manage Customers - Add, View, Edit, Delete).
- **CRUD Entity 3:** `FR-03` (Manage Items - Add, View, Edit, Delete).
- **Non-CRUD Behavior 4:** `FR-04` (Domain-based Authentication & Routing).
- **Non-CRUD Behavior 5:** `FR-05` (B2B Supplier Feedback Submission Workflow).
- **Non-CRUD Behavior 6:** `FR-06` (Cross-Domain Request Modification & Deletion Alerts).
- **Non-CRUD Behavior 7:** `FR-07` (Invalid Login Security Handling & Redirection).

**Verdict:** 100% Compliant with SE3002 assignment constraints.

### 3.2 Analysis of AI-Assisted Assumptions
1. **SRS-Supported Assumptions (FR-01, FR-03, FR-04, FR-07, NFR-01, NFR-02, NFR-03):** These match exact specifications, use case triggers, and performance thresholds from the 2008 Ejada VIPER document.
2. **Design Decision Assumptions (FR-02, FR-06):** In the 2008 SRS, the notification channel was described as *"not yet determined"* (SRS Use Case #5 Related Info). Implementing an in-app reactive notification feed with audit logs provides a modern, testable implementation basis.
3. **Unsupported / Gap Assumptions (FR-05):** The AI development tool generated the feedback form without input range validation on deliverable quantities. Rather than retroactively patching this in the baseline, this behavior was intentionally preserved to provide an authentic, reproducible test failure (**TC-07 / VIPER-BUG-01**) conforming to Part 3B requirements.
